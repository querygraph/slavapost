Walking through Bergen, Norway during 
 [CESSDA ERIC](https://no.linkedin.com/company/cessda?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-mention)
  50 years anniversary conference, surrounded by the fjords and the long tradition of European cooperation, and watching football matches with friends from different countries, I found myself reflecting on a series of discussions about Europe's future. What struck me most was the sense that we are reaching a turning point. Decisions that once seemed purely technological are now becoming strategic questions about AI sovereignty, trust, and the kind of digital society and collaboration we want to build.
